Private notes

Security

  • No passwords. You sign in with your Claude account, so this portal never sees or stores a password. Nobody can read one here, the owner included.
  • Only invited people get in. The page is private. Only people the owner shares it with can open it, and they must be signed in.
  • Only the owner decides. The database lets only the owner approve, decline or remove members, and only the owner can read requests and the activity log.
  • Your request is yours. You can read and update your own request. You cannot see anyone else's.
  • Private notes stay private. Each member's notes are readable by that member alone, the owner included.
  • Trying to get at other people's data gets you blocked. The database refuses those attempts. Anyone the owner catches trying is blocked for at least four months, and after that only the owner can lift the block.
  • What approval unlocks. The plans and documents above are the same ones on the public website. Approval opens the member tools; it does not protect secret files, so never put ID numbers or bank details in this portal.
