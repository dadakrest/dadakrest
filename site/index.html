Questions we get

Straight answers, including the inconvenient ones.

We're too small to be a target. Why bother?

Small businesses are targeted precisely because they're small — attacks are automated and indiscriminate, and smaller organisations tend to have fewer defences. Nobody picked you personally. A scanner found an open door.

Do you do penetration testing?

Not yet. Pentesting is a specialist discipline and we won't sell it until we can do it to a standard worth paying for — it's the direction this business is growing, and we'd rather tell you that honestly than overstate it. If you need a pentest today, ask us and we'll refer you to someone who does it properly. Most small businesses have gaps in the fundamentals that a pentest would simply confirm at ten times the price.

What does it cost?

Assessments are quoted at a fixed price after the free call, so the number you're given is the number you pay. Ongoing support is optional and month-to-month — there's no annual contract.

Will you need access to our systems?

For some of the work, yes. We'll tell you exactly what access is needed and why before anything happens, use the minimum required, and hand back or revoke it when the work is finished. Anything we find stays confidential.

We already have an IT person. Do we need you too?

Often not — and if your IT support already handles patching, backups and access properly, we'll say so on the call and you'll have spent nothing. Where we're useful is that IT support and security are different jobs; keeping things running and keeping things safe pull in different directions, and a second set of eyes catches what familiarity misses.
